# ⚡ Mr.V | Classroom Sync PRO

> **The Ultimate Google Classroom Auto-Sync Extension for Students & Developers.**  
> Automatically detects, organizes, and downloads all your university lectures, lab tutorials, and assignments into structured folders in seconds.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🚀 **1-Click Deep Sync**: Downloads all PDFs, slides, and documents from any Google Classroom course page with one click.
- 🧠 **Smart Folder Categorization**: Automatically detects and sorts files into:
  - 📚 `01_Theory_Lectures`
  - 🧪 `02_Practical_Labs`
  - 📝 `03_Assignments`
  - 📌 `04_Resources_and_Notes`
- 🛡️ **Interactive Review Modal**: Review all discovered materials on-page and choose what to download before syncing.
- 📁 **Customizable Storage Path**: Save to `Downloads`, `Desktop`, `Documents`, or your local server (`htdocs / CS-Stage4`).
- 💻 **Zero Server Required (Standalone Mode)**: Works out-of-the-box on **any computer** with Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, or Opera.
- 🎨 **Modern Clean UI**: Lightweight, minimalist design with Lucide vector icons.

## 🛠️ Easy Step-by-Step Setup Guide

Follow these 4 simple steps to install the extension on your laptop or PC:

### Step 1: Download the Extension
1. Click the green **Code** button at the top of this GitHub page.
2. Click **Download ZIP**.
3. Extract the ZIP file anywhere on your computer (e.g., Desktop or Documents).

---

### Step 2: Open Extensions in Your Browser
Open your browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, or Opera):
* **In Chrome**: Type `chrome://extensions` in the address bar and press Enter.
* **In Edge**: Type `edge://extensions` in the address bar and press Enter.
* **In Brave**: Type `brave://extensions` in the address bar and press Enter.

---

### Step 3: Enable Developer Mode
Look at the top-right corner of the Extensions page and turn **ON** the switch called **Developer mode**.

```text
Developer mode  [  ● ON  ]
```

---

### Step 4: Load the Extension
1. Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top-left corner.
2. Select the extracted repository folder (`Mr.V-ClassRoom-Extension`).
3. 🎉 **Done!** You will see **Mr.V | Classroom Sync PRO** added to your browser.

---

## 🎯 How to Use

1. **Pin the Extension**: Click the puzzle piece icon (🧩) in your browser toolbar and pin **Mr.V | Classroom Sync PRO**.
2. **Open Google Classroom**: Go to [classroom.google.com](https://classroom.google.com) and open any of your courses.
3. **Click the Extension**: Click the **Mr.V** icon in your toolbar.
4. **Click "Sync Current Course"**:
   - The scanner will find all course materials.
   - An on-screen checklist will appear allowing you to select/deselect files and change categories.
   - Click **Sync Selected**.
5. **Check Your Files**: All your files will be neatly organized inside your `Downloads/CS-Stage4/[Course_Name]/` folder!

---

## ⚙️ Advanced: Local XAMPP Sync (Optional for Developers)

If you have XAMPP installed and want files saved directly to `c:\xampp\htdocs\CS-Stage4`:
1. Start **Apache** in XAMPP Control Panel.
2. Place `api_sync.php` inside `C:\xampp\htdocs\CS-Stage4\api_sync.php`.
3. Open the extension popup — it will show **"Local XAMPP Workspace Connected"**.
4. Files will be placed directly into your local workspace directory.
